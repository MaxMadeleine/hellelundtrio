# Helle Lund Trio — notes from the old site

Captured 23 September 2026 from the public site https://hellelundtrio.dk/.
The HTML was last changed 23 January 2018. Use this file when building the new design. Full Danish copy is in `content.md`.

## What the old site is

One long page, built in Adobe Muse. The menu does not open other pages. It scrolls to four anchors:

- Forside — `#home`
- Om trioen — `#omtrioen`
- Projekter — `#projekter`
- Kontakt — `#kontakt`

There is no blog, no concert calendar, and no shop. Concerts are only a link to Facebook. The three “CV” links are not real CVs. They expand the short bio that is already on the band photo (about four lines each).

## Who it is for

A booking site for a North Jutland jazz trio. The visitor should be able to:

1. See who plays.
2. Understand the sound and what can be booked (concert, reception, party, workshop, lessons).
3. Hear and see past work (two CDs, YouTube clips, press photos).
4. Call, text, or mail.

## Sections to keep

1. **Hero.** Black-and-white photo of Helle at the piano. Gold title “HELLE LUND TRIO”. A small gold triangle scrolls down.
2. **Band.** One wide black-and-white photo. Names and short bios sit on the photo. Gold line under the photo.
3. **About.** Three columns: Om trioen, Vi tilbyder, Book os til (Koncert / Reception / Fest).
4. **Projects.** Concerts (Facebook only), two CD covers, a video grid, a short project history (2011–2015), press photos.
5. **Contact.** Phone and mail over a dark photo. “Ring, sms eller mail.”

## Look

- Almost everything is black, white, and one gold.
- Gold used for the title, menu, section labels, and rules: `#D0940D`.
- Page background is white. Hero and contact sit on dark photos.
- Photos of the band are black and white. CD covers are in color.
- A gray floral swirl sits behind the about text and in the corner of the hero. It is decoration, not a logo.
- Headings are wide, tracked-out, uppercase. Body text is a plain sans-serif, left aligned, quite small.
- Fonts loaded on the old site: Advent Pro (titles), Source Sans Pro, Muli, Open Sans.
- The page is desktop-first. On a narrow window the Muse layout stacks poorly. The new site should be built for a phone first.

## Words worth keeping

The line that describes the music:

> Helle Lund Trio spiller moderne trio-jazz med et stærkt personligt udtryk og et strejf af nordisk tone.

Booking types already written: koncert, reception, fest, jam-session, workshop, sammenspil og improvisation, solo (piano / bas / trommer).

Tone of the copy is calm and practical. No slogans. Short sentences. Danish only.

## Projects named on the site

- 2012–13: Aalborg Jazz Connection, with Swing Time Big Band.
- 2014–2015: Danske Årstidssange, with Finn Møller, Klaverstemmeren, Aalborg. Workshops at Center for Dansk Jazzhistorie, Musikkens Hus. Church concerts. Care-home tour in Aalborg Kommune in 2015.
- Support named: Jazzdanmark, MUSAM, Kulturpuljen i Aalborg Kommune, Det Obelske Familiefond, Nordeafonden. From 2014, concerts with KUNSTEN, Aalborg.
